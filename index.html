Orgin story......the pionners of the first mediival video game include developers Roy Trubshaw and Richard Bartle: In 1978, they created MUD (Multi-User Dungeon), the first multiplayer online virtual world. It relied heavily on medieval fantasy tropes........Later in the 1980s, specifically 1986, Kellyn Beeck created "Defender of the crown." Set in 1149 medieval England during a civil war, it became famous for its jaw-dropping graphics on the Amiga and allowed players to engage in jousting, castle sieges, and sword fighting.
